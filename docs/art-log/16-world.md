# Art log: Tactical world performance and density

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md section 16 on 2026-09-25. Numbers are as recorded at the time.

`is_position_walkable` is answered through a 512-unit obstacle broadphase grid rather than a linear scan. It sits on the authoritative path - `resolve_player_motion` makes up to three calls per player per 20 Hz tick - so with two seats the scan over all 461 obstacles cost roughly 25 ms of every wall-clock second. Obstacles stamp their zero-clearance world bounds into the grid, but a rotated rectangle expands along its own local axes, so a query pads by `clearance * sqrt(2)` to stay a strict superset of what the exact test could accept. Measured over 6,000 queries the scan runs at 209.9 us and the grid at 3.8 us, a 55.2x reduction with 0 mismatches against a brute-force reference. The sampling is the load-bearing part of that proof: half the points were drawn inside an obstacle's own padded bounds, giving 34.1 percent actually blocked, because uniform sampling of a 28,672-unit world puts almost every point where the pad cannot be wrong and would let a broken grid score a clean run.

The ground-cover family is the ecology layer beneath that: 3,245 clusters and 38,947 elements, batched at two draw commands per cluster across 170 render chunks, gated by biome, walkability, road verge, and the camp clearance, and drawn with no material at all. Materiallessness was checked rather than assumed - it is not the `render_mode unshaded` opt-out that exempts a shader subject from the night `CanvasModulate`, and measured across the day and night captures the blade tips darken at ratio 0.631 against the ground's 0.630 and the frame's 0.633. Its density was also nearly retuned on a stale complaint. The observation that only about five elements read per screen was true of the first colour pass and false afterwards; the fix had been to author each kind backwards from the measured floor so it presents a value pair rather than a tint, and the current gameplay capture carries 4.42 percent of its pixels at least 18 luma above the modal ground and 7.10 percent at least 8 below it across 2,761 lit runs. Tripling the cluster count would have multiplied something that was never missing, so the constants stand and the placement cost stays at 192.5 ms of world build, down from 1,908 ms before the broadphase.

## 2026-09-25 polish pass and Phases 2-3

The plan, the commits and the before/after table are in [`docs/POLISH_PLAN_2026-09-25.md`](../POLISH_PLAN_2026-09-25.md). This entry keeps what was learned along the way.

**The capture set did not show the player's view.** Twenty-three of the twenty-five world captures are framed between zoom 0.0092 and 0.45 to read layout, and only the two wilderness-ecology frames used the shipped 0.38. Every defect this pass fixed was invisible at layout zoom and obvious at 0.38. `gameplay_tour_render_validation` now captures 25 stops at exactly 0.38, with a baked Heikki in every frame as a scale reference and each stop resolved from live nodes, so a relayout moves the camera rather than leaving a stale frame. The measurement it made first was emptiness: most stops were one or two subjects on a whole screen of ground.

**World units are not the delivered unit.** The Ostari foundation had a 26-unit near-black frame and a pale inner stroke. The district crack was an 18-unit bar with an 85-unit branch out of its side. At 0.38 those became a 10-pixel UI border and a 7-pixel stick. This is the same species of defect as the texel-authored outline in section 8, now arriving on vector primitives. The lot now wears the terrain's own concrete grain through the road grain shader and fades into a rubble berm at its edge. Cracks go through `GroundFissure`, which tapers to hairline tips.

**A groove and a stick differ by where their light falls.** A dark stroke on the ground reads as a dropped stick because a stick casts its shadow down-right while its top catches the light. `GroundFissure` puts a faint *lit* lip down-right of the dark core, which is the reverse, and that reverse is what makes a stroke read as cut into the ground rather than lying on it. Meander and taper help, but without the lip a fissure still read as a twig.

**Density could not come from colliding trees.** Metsä places three or four colliding stands per 2,048-unit cell, about one per screen. Each trunk is rasterised into the flow field with 215 units of clearance, so adding trunks would have closed the horde's routes. The density came from two visual-only layers configured after the canonical pipeline on their own seed streams, owning no physics or flow. The flow hash is the proof that they touch nothing: it held across the change. **A density fix that adds collision is a navigation change; add visual-only layers instead.**

**A procedural shrub read as a mossy rock.** The first understory shrubs were drawn blobs, and at 30 pixels a round green blob with a dark rim reads as a boulder. Cropping the two approved dense firs above the bole gave a foliage silhouette without promoting any unreviewed wild frame.

**A new visual layer can take a host pocket from a gate.** The understory's forest fill covered wilderness pockets that `existing_wild_atlas_context_validation` needs clear to host its trial card: three pockets went unhostable against the gate's cap of one, then two after the wilderness fill was cut to 0.24. Later, the spur shifted the ambient layer's sequential stream into pocket 4 and took that one as well. The understory now leaves the composition's wilderness pockets to the layers that dress them, and both card gates count it as an ambient-family envelope.

**A clone is caught by a translation test and a mirror test.** East Kylät was West translated by about +16,100. The gate compares every east house and wall position with the west's, both translated by +16,100 and mirrored across x = 0, and counts matches within 400 units. It allows at most one of each and currently finds none. The mirror test is there because mirroring is the obvious cheap way to fake a second layout.

**Draw order decides which layer owns a junction.** The camp clearing was first drawn above the roads, so it painted over the spur's end and the camp showed no exit. Moved under the roads, the spur can be seen leaving it. The same rule fixed the central cross: every shoulder is now drawn before any bed, so the north-south shoulder no longer lies across the east-west carriageway. A round cap on a free road end read as a landing pad; a shoulder that tapers onto the bed and a ragged cap that fades toward its tip read as a road wearing out.

**Occupancy counts detail, not quality.** The share of pixels departing more than 14 luma from their 61-pixel mean went *down* on the camp (17.6 to 13.4 percent) and the junction (17.5 to 12.9) because those fixes removed noise. Occupancy finds empty frames. It cannot say whether a frame got better, so every row in the plan's table carries a verdict taken off the frame.

**Godot's `orthogonal()` points outward on a clockwise y-down polygon.** The berm and spill first grew into the lot rather than out of it. For a polygon wound clockwise in screen space, `edge.orthogonal()` is the outward normal and its negation the inward one.
